## Brand & Style
The design system fuses the disciplined craftsmanship of Apple Human Interface Guidelines with mission-critical enterprise financial intelligence. Tailored for enterprise merchants, institutional treasury leads, and executive founders, the interface establishes an atmosphere of absolute fiscal authority, crystal-clear predictive insight, and fluid responsiveness.

The visual style synthesizes frosted glassmorphism with high-fidelity corporate modernism. Interfaces leverage layered translucent planes, micro-diffused ambient depth, hairline specular highlights, and organic squircle geometry. The experience rejects noisy ornaments in favor of structural clarity, optical balance, and frictionless cognitive flow.

## Colors
The color hierarchy is anchored in deep sovereign sapphire (`#002E6E`), reflecting institutional security and regulatory trust. Paytm cyan (`#00BAF2`) functions as the primary interaction and confirmation layer, while electric cyan (`#00D2FF`) is reserved for high-velocity predictive AI vectors, machine learning triggers, and dynamic trend lines.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Surface Base**: Pure titanium white (`#FFFFFF`) underlaid with ultra-soft cool slate canvas (`#F4F7FB`).
- **Glass Materials**: Translucent milk fills (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`) with dynamic background filters.
- **Specular Accents**: Hairline inner ring and border edge strokes (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.60)` on top/left, `rgba(0, 46, 110, 0.08)` on bottom/right).
- **Ink & Neutral Tokens**:
  - Primary Typography: Ultra-deep obsidian navy (`#0A0F1D`).
  - Secondary Typography: Polished steel (`#4A5568`).
  - Tertiary / Supporting: Muted slate (`#8A94A6`).
- **Semantic Status Signals**:
  - Yield Positive: Emerald jade (`#00A86B`) paired with soft emerald mist (`#E6F7F0`).
  - Risk / Action Required: Crimson coral (`#E53935`) paired with tinted quartz (`#FDECEC`).
  - Predictive Computing: Luminescent cyan gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #002E6E 0%, #00BAF2 70%, #00D2FF 100%)`).

## Typography
Typographic rhythm relies on tightly controlled tracking, vertical metric consistency, and contextual weight modulation reminiscent of Apple's SF Pro architecture.

- **Tabular Figures**: Force tabular figures (`tnum`) across all monetary tables, ledger strips, telemetry dials, and settlement volumes to guarantee perfect vertical column alignment.
- **Negative Tracking**: Apply systematic negative letter spacing to all headline levels to eliminate visual gaps in large typography, preserving a compact, executive aesthetic.
- **Micro-Hierarchy**: Label badges mandate uppercase formatting with relaxed letter-spacing (`0.04em`) to contrast cleanly against natural case numerical metrics.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs an iOS-derived fluid structure with rigid exterior margins and content-bounded modular stacks. 

### Grid System
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column layout with fixed fluid boundaries (max width: `1440px`), `2.5rem` canvas margin, and `1.5rem` internal gutters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8-column layout, `1.5rem` canvas margin, and `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: Single-column / 4-column sub-grid, `1rem` safe-area canvas margin, and `0.75rem` gutters.

### Component Spacing Rhythm
- Internal card padding standardizes at `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) on desktop and automatically compresses to `space-md` (`1rem`) on viewport sizes under 640px.
- Vertical section orchestration strictly adheres to multiples of `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) to create intentional spatial breathing room between autonomous AI widget groupings.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is generated through translucent glass material tiers, optical refraction, and multi-stop ambient light modeling rather than opaque shadows.

### Glass Surfaces (Vibrancy)
- **Primary Canvas**: Solid foundation (`#F4F7FB`).
- **Glass Sheet (Cards & Modules)**: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78)` coupled with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(180%)`.
- **Raised Glass (Flyouts, Sheets, Modals)**: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` coupled with `backdrop-filter: blur(36px) saturate(200%)`.

### Layered Specular Borders
Every glass container utilizes an ultra-fine 1px perimeter composed of a compound directional linear stroke:
- Top-to-bottom border: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.70)` to `rgba(215, 226, 238, 0.40)`.
- Eliminates murky outlines and mimics real-world beveled glass refraction.

### Ambient Shadow Hierarchy
- **Level 1 (Docked Module)**: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(0, 46, 110, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`
- **Level 2 (Active Card / Hover)**: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 46, 110, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`
- **Level 3 (Modal / Floating Intelligence Tray)**: `0 24px 60px -8px rgba(0, 46, 110, 0.14), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`

## Shapes
Geometry is informed by continuous curvature (Apple squircle language), removing abrupt corners in favor of fluid geometric arcs.

- **Primary Module Containers**: Evaluated at continuous `24px` (`rounded-3xl` equivalent) for primary metric surfaces and AI recommendation panels.
- **Embedded Inner Cards**: Standardized at `16px` (`rounded-2xl` equivalent) to guarantee exact geometric concentricity inside parent containers.
- **Micro UI Elements (Pills, Action Buttons, Badges)**: Rendered either as `10px` or full radial capsules (`9999px`) for quick-scan intelligence metadata and filter chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Direct / Settle)**: Liquid gradient base (`linear-gradient(135deg, #002E6E 0%, #004299 100%)`), white typography, 1px top highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`), inner border, and soft sapphire drop shadow (`0 8px 20px -4px rgba(0, 46, 110, 0.25)`). Active state applies a scale transform (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Glass Action**: Translucent white fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`), 1px solid stroke (`rgba(0, 46, 110, 0.12)`), text `#002E6E`. Blur `12px`.
- **AI Assist Action**: Luminescent electric cyan border highlight with a subtle cyclic pulse (`rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.35)`).

### Input Fields
- Surface: Glass fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`), `16px` border radius, 1px perimeter stroke (`rgba(0, 46, 110, 0.1)`).
- Focus State: Border transitions to `#00BAF2` backed by a multi-layered focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 186, 242, 0.18)`).
- Integrated AI Prompt Prefix: Features an iridescent icon glyph with animated gradient shimmer when processing queries.

### Cards & Intelligence Panels
- Multi-surface frosted glass card containing an upper telemetry bar, real-time volume display, and an bottom insight rail.
- Bottom rail is visually anchored with an isolated backdrop tint (`rgba(0, 186, 242, 0.04)`) and separated by a 1px border (`rgba(0, 46, 110, 0.05)`).

### Filter & Segmented Chips
- Segmented control mimics iOS tactile trays: recessed container (`rgba(0, 46, 110, 0.05)`) with an absolute floating glass thumb (`#FFFFFF` with Level 1 shadow) snapping dynamically across segments.
- Standalone Filter Chips: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `8px 16px`, subtle border, switching to `#002E6E` fill with crisp white text when selected.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Smooth squircles (`6px` radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios).
- Inactive: Frosted inset fill with `1.5px` border (`rgba(0, 46, 110, 0.2)`).
- Active: Solid `#00BAF2` fill featuring a crisp white SVG check or inner radio dot, transitioning via quick spring mechanics (`cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`).

### AI Dynamic Notification Strip
- Docked or floating pill containing a mini live sparkline, settlement anomaly detection score, and micro CTA.
- Uses specular edge glow (`0 0 16px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.25)`) to signal active computational monitoring without obstructing transactional screens.
